# NexGen World-Class ERP/POS Module Rebuild

This iteration applies a shared, operational SaaS design language across:
- Purchases
- Expenses
- Easy Return
- Categories
- Brands
- Units
- Products
- Customers
- Customer Payments
- Customer Ledger
- Suppliers
- Supplier Payments
- Supplier Ledger
- Reports & Analytics
- Settings & Administration

Principles:
- Readability first
- Clear hierarchy
- Calm enterprise density
- Reusable UI primitives
- Real API/business logic preserved
- Form sections visually grouped
- Tables treated as work surfaces
- Responsive behavior improved without replacing business workflows
- Modals and forms receive a consistent world-class treatment

No mock business data was introduced by this iteration.
